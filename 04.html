Box #4

Makeup

I never really wore a ton of makeup in high school, but as I go to college I think it'd be really nice to get to know myself- starting with the basics. Makeup helps you understand your features; how they balance one another and make you unique. I want to work with the shadows and highlights, and find which colors suit me best. And of course, putting myself to together in the morning is the best way to motivate me through the day.
