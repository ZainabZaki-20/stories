Box #4

Music

Living in a house of six, it was rare that the house was ever quite. And with my old room being on the opposite side of the TV, I'd always hear conversation, music, a tv, or even just the birds outside my window. In my new apartment, there is a lot less going on and my room is seperated from my roomates so I know I will miss that background noise. That's why I should bring CDs from my favorite artists! Some bollywood to remind me of car drives, 2010 hits to remind me of living room dance parties, and maybe something more recent as I discover what I like to listen to.
