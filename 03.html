As Escape From Reality

Box #3

Fantasy Books

Ever since I was a kid, on the playground or on the bus, I'd always have my head in a book. It'd take me out of the dreary day or bumpy ride and replace my ground with something filled with magic and mystery. Though college is something new and exciting, I know there will be times where I am stressed or begin doubting myself. I hope that the collection of my favorite stories will help me be able to put these engative thoughts aside by providing a momentary escape.
