Box #3 As Old As Me

Fantasy Books

Ever since I was a kid, on the playground or on the bus, I'd always have my head in a book. It'd take me out of the bumpy ride or dreary day and replace my ground with something filled with magic and mystery. Though college is something new and exciting, I know there will be times where I am stressed or begin doubting myself. i hope that the collection of my favorite stories will be able to put these negative thoughts on pause by taking me back to what I love most so then I can continue stronger.
