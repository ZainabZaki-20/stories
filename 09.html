Box #9

Art

I remember all throughout high school going back and forth about if I'd be able to pursue art as a career. I knew I loved it as a hobbie, alongside writing but was it enough to turn it into a lifestyle? Through my senior year I really put these hobbies to a test and solidified how much they meant to me. So now, I want to surround my new room with my favorite art to remind myself why I committed to this degree, and how much I can achieve with it.
