Beauty Is In The Eye Of The Beholder

Box #11

Dead Roses

My parents have always been plant lovers, growing roses in our front yards, and mint on our walkway. When the roses would be in prime bloom, my dad would cut the roses out and put them in vases for my mom. What surprised me the most as a kid was that once the roses died, my mom wouldn't get rid of them, in fact she'd keep them. I want to keep this tradition in my apartment as well to remember that beauty is not always as it seems.
