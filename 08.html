Kurtis

All throughout grade school I was so afriad to wear anything that would even signal toward my culture or religion because I was so afraid of what people would think. On the other hand, my brother would proudly bring julaab jamaan instead of cupcakes for his birthday, and would proudly wear a kurta on picture day. He's inspired me so much to now embrace that culture and fall in love with it. In this new enviornment I want to promise myself to express my true self and be in what I am comfortable in, not what is expected of me.
