Camera & Poloroids

All this time I'd taken photos on my phone but it isn't the same as having a phsyical copy. I love how a poloroid is able to capture a moment with a nostalgic filter but also stops you from trying to edit the photo, leaving it in a raw status to represent the moment. I hope to take a lot of photos in college and put them up on my wall to commemorate a lot of meaningful moments.
