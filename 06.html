All You Need Is A Good Base

Box #6

Rice

While South Asian food is very extravagant, it stems off of humble ingredients like rice, grain, tomatos, and onions. I always helped my mom cook these meals and wonder how the same base can create such a variety of flavors and make my day? It was something she was dead-set on teaching me before I left, and now I carry those same ingredients. I know I won't be able to have home-cooked meals everyday, but if I can try, then I can manage to gain that same satisfaction by making it myself.
