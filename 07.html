Box #7

Perfume

Since middle school, I've connected a segment of my life to a perfume. For example, high school was very fruity floral because I put myself out there and began being myself rather than conforming to the 'ideal type'. Now with college, I bought a new perfume, one I've had my eye on but waited to get until I felt as mature and elegant as the scent itself. I not only hope that this scent carries this energy with me from day to day, but that I can tie good memories to it.
